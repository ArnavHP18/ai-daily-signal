### How to use this alongside the Signal
- **Speed accounts** (@tier10k, @testingcatalog, @vxunderground, the sports insiders) break news first but are often wrong or unconfirmed. Use them as alerts, not facts, which is the same rule the edition applies to rumours.
- **Official accounts** are the ones to check before acting on a story, the same "Official source" level the edition marks on cards.
- **Start small** if 200+ accounts is too many: Banking, Models, Security and Markets cover most of what you act on day to day.
