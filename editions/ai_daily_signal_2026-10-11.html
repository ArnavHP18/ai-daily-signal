■ Today's Lead · New on the Wire · Dated Thu 8 Oct · Day 5 of the agent-workplace-identity arc

Google gives AI agents their own email addresses, calendars and Drive accounts, and lets its Gemini agent hand work to Anthropic's Claude

At Gemini at Work 2026 (Thu 8 Oct) Google launched a single Gemini agent for businesses. It can spin up sub-agents, each with its own identity, for multi-step jobs that run "for hours or days". It can also create persistent "coworker agents" with their own @agents.company.com email, calendar, Drive storage and directory listing. Their actions are logged under the agent's identity, not an employee's. The agent routes each task across the Gemini family and Anthropic's Claude models, with other models to follow (Google Cloud (primary) · TechCrunch · Bloomberg · Decrypt).

So what: this is the first hyperscaler to treat agents as staff in the identity directory: provisioned, permissioned, audited and given an inbox. IT and security teams on Workspace now have to decide who approves an agent's account, what it may open and who answers for its mail. Missed for three days by narrower scans; dated honestly as 8 October.

Identity model. Google says every agent gets "its own identity, cryptographically attested and governed like an employee, with least-privilege permissions". The identity is stamped into logs and into any VM the agent starts. Agents run in an Agent Sandbox with its own network boundary, and all traffic passes through an Agent Gateway that Google calls an "AI network firewall". External connections map identity via OAuth. The agent can also connect to any MCP server inside or outside the company network.

Reach. Accessible from web, iOS, Android, Windows, Mac, the command line, Workspace, Microsoft 365 and Slack, or headless inside other apps. Financial Services and Legal specialisations are in preview; Government, Healthcare and Retail are "coming soon". TokenPost and TechCrunch report the universal agent is in private preview for select Gemini Enterprise subscribers. No price was given; Google added per-project real-time spend caps that pause an agent when hit.

Vendor claims, not verified. Nearly 500 Google Cloud customers each processed over a trillion tokens; nearly 90% of the Fortune 100 use Gemini Enterprise; Orange Spain runs 1,000+ custom agents and SOMPO 10,000+; PayPal routes 10 million multi-model requests a week.

Why Claude matters here. Google is now a distribution channel for Anthropic models inside its own flagship agent, the same week Meta and Microsoft were reported to be cutting internal Claude use (New #5).

Not yet known. GA date, pricing, whether agent accounts count as paid Workspace seats, and how the agents identify themselves to outside businesses: the job the Personal Agent Protocol (Developing D1) is trying to standardise.

IMPACT 6/10#2 MODELS · WORLD MODELS · EMERGING TECH · WATCHLIST CORRECTION · DATED THU 8 OCT

Odyssey ships Odyssey-3, a real-time foundation world model that claims a state-of-the-art 66.1 on Physics-IQ Verified and is already driving robots, with a free browser preview

Odyssey-3 is an autoregressive diffusion transformer. It predicts in real time how a scene evolves given past frames and the latest actions or injected events. Odyssey says the Pro (720p) tier scores 66.1 on Physics-IQ Verified video-to-video, the highest reported (self-reported), and leads 3 of 4 WorldMark categories. Demonstrated uses: controlling robot arms and humanoids, driving, piloting drones, generating training environments for agents, and games. Odyssey names humanoid-autonomy firm Flexion as an adopter. Access: free research preview in the browser; API by request; no open weights or public price (Business Wire via StreetInsider (primary) · Odyssey on X · AlphaSignal · AICoder).

Novel-approach bonus: one learned simulator used for both robot control and agent training, shipped publicly 23 days after its September unveiling. Correction: the watchlist recorded "Odyssey-3: nothing found" on 9 and 10 October; it had launched on the 8th. Physics-IQ is a vendor claim until reproduced.

IMPACT 6/10#3 RESEARCH · JEPA · WORLD MODELS · WATCHLIST CORRECTION · DATED 6–8 OCT

AMI Labs' first public research result: H-JEPA, a hierarchical world model that plans at three levels of abstraction and lifts Visual AntMaze success from 18% to 73% with less planner compute, with code released

H-JEPA (arXiv 2610.06805; Zhang, Terver, Rabbat, LeCun, Balestriero; AMI Labs with NYU, INRIA Paris and Brown). It learns separate latent representations at different timescales. Each level's predictions become sub-goals for the planner below. A three-level hierarchy takes Visual AntMaze success from 18% to 73% using less planning compute than a flat JEPA; code is on GitHub (arXiv · VentureBeat, 8 Oct · GitHub). Separately, JEPA-Anything (arXiv 2609.20800, PhAI Labs with Stanford, Oxford and Princeton) trains one JEPA world model across seven domains from molecular dynamics to weather. Its biology factor nominated an intervention (reported as IL-18 with CD73 blockade) that was then tested in organoids and mice. That is a preprint, not a therapy (The Decoder, 6 Oct · arXiv).

The watchlist's lead non-LLM approach now has public, reproducible results from LeCun's lab, not just a $1.03B seed. Correction: the 8–10 October watchlist notes said AMI Labs had no paper. Watch for H-JEPA on real robots and any AMI product timeline.

IMPACT 6/10#4 MODELS · DECISION MODELS · OPEN WEIGHTS · DATED FRI 9 OCT

Cloudflare releases Clef-omni, an Apache-2.0 decision model that takes audio and video as well as text and images, and cuts Clef-flash to $0.038 per million input tokens, under Jev's price

Clef-omni is a 30B mixture-of-experts (3B active) built on Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct with the speech-output parts removed. Like Jev, it does not generate text: it scores typed questions and returns a probability per allowed answer. It accepts text, JSON, images, WAV/MP3 audio and MP4/WebM video, removing the transcription or frame-extraction step before a decision. Weights are on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0; it is served on Workers AI and listed on OpenRouter. In the same release, Clef-flash fell to $0.038/M input and Clef got up to 2x faster. Latency and hosted-context figures differ between sources (Cloudflare blog (primary) · changelog · Hugging Face · AIBase).

The decision-model price floor is now set by an open model at $0.038/M, below Jev and Microsoft-Decision-1 ($0.042/M). Multimodal decisions, such as "is this call a complaint?" or "does this clip break policy?", are a new use class.

Research beat: Xiaomi publishes how it scaled RL on MiMo-V2.6 and open-sources 7,000+ RL task environments and the training framework, at a reported ~$3.5M of compute

Technical report "MiMo-V2.6: Scaling Reinforcement Learning Towards Self-Improvement" (arXiv 2610.11959).
