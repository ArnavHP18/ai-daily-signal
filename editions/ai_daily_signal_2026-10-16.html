■ Today's Lead · New · Thu 15 Oct · Day 16 of the open-weight-ceiling arc

Meta releases the weights for Muse Spark Open, an 816B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with a 1M-token context, and calls it the most capable US open-weight model to date

On 15 October Meta put Muse-Spark-Open-816B-A42B on Hugging Face. It is a mixture-of-experts model with roughly 42B active parameters per token, a 1M-token context, multimodal input and text-only output, shipped in BF16 and NVFP4 under the Muse Model License, which allows many developers to use it at no cost but limits commercial terms. It was available on day one through SageMaker, Google Cloud Model Garden, Microsoft Foundry, and hosts including Groq, Together, Fireworks, Baseten and OpenRouter. It was also the top Hacker News story of the day, according to a third-party digest.

So what: this is Meta's largest open-weight release of the Muse era; the previous one, Muse Glimmer, was a 30B model. It gives US enterprises that will not run Chinese weights a domestic option near the top of the open tier. It does not lead that tier: VentureBeat notes an Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index of 39, against 46 for Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro, and Meta's own table puts it behind Kimi K3 and GLM 5.3 on several coding tasks. Read the licence before building on it.

Hardware. Local serving needs at least ~700GB of GPU memory in NVFP4, or about 1.5–2TB in BF16 (Meta and Hugging Face give different figures). Meta says it runs on two paired M5 Ultra Mac Studios with MLX; VentureBeat points out that this memory configuration has not shipped yet.

Safety posture. The open version received extra safety post-training so it is harder to misuse without system-level guardrails, and Meta recommends deploying it inside a broader system with its own protections. That matters because the EU AI Office is now sending information requests to labs (Tracking), and open weights cannot be recalled.

Where it ranks. All benchmark figures are Meta's, except the Artificial Analysis score VentureBeat cites. Meta claims leads on some tool-use tests. On coding and professional tasks it trails the leading Chinese open models.

Watch for. Independent agentic-coding scores; whether a burst of quantisations and fine-tunes appears on Hugging Face; licence terms for large commercial users; and whether open-weight policy debates in Washington cite the release.

IMPACT 6/10#2 ENTERPRISE · APPLIED DEEP BEAT · BANKING · AML · THU 15 OCT

FIS's Financial Crimes AI Agent, built on Anthropic's Claude, reaches US banks for anti-money-laundering investigations; Amalgamated Bank is beginning to deploy it

Announced with Anthropic in May for general availability in the second half of 2026, with BMO and Amalgamated as development partners. The agent pulls evidence from a bank's core systems, matches activity against known typologies, ranks the highest-risk alerts and drafts Suspicious Activity Report narratives. Investigators keep the final decision. FIS says client data stays on FIS-controlled infrastructure and every agent step is auditable. Forbes (15 Oct) reports it is now commercially available; Amalgamated's projected cuts in alert-review and SAR-drafting time are the bank's own estimates.

AML is one of banking's largest compliance cost centres, and FIS sits under thousands of US banks' core systems. A frontier-lab model distributed through a core processor is the fastest route for agents into community banking. It is also the use case US bank supervisors are now asking about (Tracking).

IMPACT 5/10#3 RESEARCH · EMERGING TECH · WORLD MODELS · LAUNCH BLOG ~12 OCT (PAPER: AUG)

Helix World (Noiz AI and HKUST): a real-time interactive world model that generates video and spatial stereo audio together, conditioned on the user's movement

From one image, the user moves with WASD and looks around with the arrow keys. The model generates the next frames and matching 48kHz stereo audio in one Transformer, at 24 fps on one H800, and the sound field shifts as the camera turns. Developer-reported results: WBench navigation 79.9 (real-time factor 0.77); on its own HelixBench, spatial-acoustic consistency 41.8 against 12.7 for EchoWM. The paper, code, a preview checkpoint on Hugging Face and a browser demo are public. The arXiv paper dates to August; trackers date the launch to 12 Oct.

Watchlist movement (world models), and a correction: the 13–15 Oct world-model searches missed this. Adding audio to world models is a new branch. Sound carries material, distance and contact cues that matter for robots and games. All scores so far are the developers' own; a new watchlist entry tracks the class.

IMPACT 5/10#4 MODELS · EMERGING TECH · DECISION MODELS · V1 1 OCT / V1.1 6 OCT

Perplexity's Decider, a 27B decision model fine-tuned from Qwen3.8-27B, is on OpenRouter's Decisions API; V1.1 adds image input

Instead of generating text, it returns probabilities for typed questions: yes/no, multiple choice and rubric scores, with up to 128 questions per request about the same content. It is aimed at classification, routing, moderation and grading. It does not work with chat-completions SDKs. Trackers disagree on the price ($0.02–0.04/M input, output free), and one says the weights are Apache 2.0 on Hugging Face; neither point is confirmed by Perplexity docs. OpenRouter's models page lists a 'Decider Latest' dated 14 Oct.

Watchlist movement (decision models), missed by scans since 1 Oct. A consumer-AI company now ships its own decision model through a gateway. That makes at least six vendors (TypeSafe, OpenAI, Microsoft, Cloudflare, Amazon, Perplexity) selling the format, and pushes input prices for it toward zero.

IMPACT 4/10#7 POLICY · US STATE LAW · LEGAL PROFESSION · SIGNED 30 SEP

California SB 574 writes rules for lawyers' use of generative AI into statute, effective 1 January 2027

SB 574 was one of more than two dozen AI and privacy bills Governor Newsom signed at the end of the 2026 session. Others include SB 1000, which amends the AI Transparency Act and took effect immediately. Law-firm alerts this month detail the obligations. Separately, Connecticut's CART Act (SB 5) began phasing in on 1 Oct. There is still no federal AI statute.

Firms using legal AI (Ironclad and Z.ai's GLM-Legal, both in Tracking) get a statutory baseline in the largest US legal market. Check the bill text before relying on summaries.

IMPACT 5/10D2 RESEARCH · AI FOR MATHEMATICS · REACTION REPORTED ~14–15 OCT

Fields Medallist Hugo Duminil-Copin says OpenAI's AI-generated mathematics 'annihilated' his research agenda

WHAT CHANGED Tracking had no movement since the 7 Oct withdrawals. New: a Fields Medallist's public account of the damage to his field, picked up by IBTimes UK and OfficeChai and ranked in a 15 Oct Hacker News top-ten digest.
The 2022 Fields Medallist said the release felt like being hit by a truck, that it solved 16 questions he had worked on for years among 377 claimed results in his area, that he cried hard enough one night to wake his daughter, and that his PhD students and postdocs are 'in total panic'. Earlier reactions: Oxford's Ursula Martin called it 'a messy first draft' left for mathematicians to clean up; the Association for Human Mathematics urged colleagues to stop working with OpenAI. Counts differ across outlets (722 manuscripts, '700+', '300 proofs'). A claim that the model solved Navier-Stokes is uncorrected and unverified, and is not repeated here.

The first case of a top researcher saying in public that an AI release ended his agenda. Funding bodies and journals will now face the question of what human mathematics is for.
