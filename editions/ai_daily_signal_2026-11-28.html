Bank of England cuts UK bank capital requirements and warns AI borrowing links risks together

Tier 1 needs fall by about 0.5 points of risk-weighted assets; hyperscaler bonds pass $305bn this year

CRITICAL · ACT THIS WEEKOfficial sourceChecked on the Bank of England's November 2026 Financial Stability Report page (opened)

The Bank of England's Financial Policy Committee published its November Financial Stability Report on 26 Nov and closed its review of bank capital. The PRA will consult on lower minimum requirements for domestic lending, which together with earlier changes cut Tier 1 capital requirements for major UK banks by about 50 basis points of risk-weighted assets in normal times, with further relief in stress scenarios. System-wide requirements will sit at the FPC's updated benchmark of around 13%, and the neutral countercyclical buffer stays at 2%. The FPC says going much further 'would risk financial instability'.

The same report warns that AI-related financing now spans equities, public bonds, private credit and 'circular' vendor-financing deals: hyperscaler bond issuance is set to exceed $305bn in 2026, about 8% of global investment-grade issuance. It says frontier AI agents and open-weight models make cyber and operational risk a more prominent threat, and asks firms to speed up quantum-safe cryptography.

So what: UK lenders get room to lend or return capital, but supervisors want to see where AI-linked exposures sit and how fast banks can recover from cyber incidents; expect questions on both in 2027 reviews.

OFFICIALBank of England: Financial Stability Report, November 2026REPORTINGFT: BoE to cut capital requirements for banks as it warns of AI risks · WSJ: BoE warns scale and breadth of AI financing poses threat to financial stability
Storyline: bank capital and risk controls · day 3

Twice a year the Bank of England's Financial Policy Committee sets out the risks to the UK financial system. This report did two things at once. It finished a review the government wanted, aimed at freeing banks to lend more: minimum requirements tied to domestic lending will fall, so the biggest UK banks need roughly half a percentage point less Tier 1 capital against their risk-weighted assets, and the committee's overall benchmark settles at about 13%. It also drew a line: cutting much further would weaken banks' ability to absorb shocks.

The risk section is where AI comes in. Borrowing to build data centres is growing fast and spreading across bonds, private credit and complex vendor-financing loops, which makes it harder to see who ultimately holds the risk. The committee also says more capable AI agents and open-weight models raise the bar for cyber defence, and that the shift to quantum-resistant cryptography should start now.

#3Security · US government · 27 NovIMPACT 8/10

Hackers breached the US Air Force cloud that runs air-defence and munitions systems

Attackers got into Cloud One, the US Air Force's shared cloud platform, by exploiting an unidentified software vulnerability, Reuters reports. So what: no attribution yet, but a run of federal breaches through shared platforms is exactly the concentration risk the Bank of England flagged the same week; expect new CISA directives.
