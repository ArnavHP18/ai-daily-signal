#4

VMware patches vCenter zero-day that Chinese hackers exploited for two months

CRITICALTwo or more outletsSecurity · patch now · 19 Nov

Broadcom released emergency fixes on 19 Nov (VMSA-2026-0009) for CVE-2026-123890 (CVSS 9.8), a flaw in the VMware Directory Service of vCenter Server that lets an unauthenticated attacker with network access get an administrator session. All vCenter versions before the fixed releases are affected, including inside VMware Cloud Foundation and vSphere Foundation; there is no workaround. Google's Mandiant, which reported it, says the China-linked group Armory Castle (formerly UNC5221) used it from early September: it got in through a BeyondTrust Remote Support zero-day, used a tool called Bonesaw to steal SAML signing material, created a single-sign-on admin account, forged tokens, installed backdoors and took the vCenter master encryption key before moving to ESXi hosts. The same advisory fixes four flaws in SDDC Manager and VMware Tools for Windows.

So what: vCenter runs the virtual machines of most large banks; patch now, then hunt for unexpected SSO admin accounts and forged SAML tokens, because a two-month head start means some networks are already compromised.

#5

ECB warns AI valuations and debt-funded data centres now threaten financial stability

NOTABLEOfficial sourceBanking · financial stability · 18 Nov

The European Central Bank's twice-yearly Financial Stability Review, published on 18 Nov, puts AI valuations alongside fiscal imbalances and geoeconomic tension as the main risks. It says equity prices depend on 'ambitious earnings expectations' that financing worries or cheaper AI models could challenge, and that AI infrastructure is 'increasingly funded through debt, such that a potential change in sentiment could spread losses from equity markets to bond markets'. Euro-area banks and non-banks are exposed through AI financing and sovereign bonds; banks are judged well placed thanks to strong profits, capital and liquidity, with vulnerabilities larger in some non-banks. The issue also has a special focus on how AI is reshaping cyber threats. Protos reports the review counts about $4.5 trillion in debt-like off-balance-sheet commitments (data-centre leases, cloud and chip contracts) at eight US tech firms, up 287% on 2025.

So what: this is the supervisor telling banks to look at AI exposure as credit risk, not just equity risk: data-centre loans, GPU-backed lending and lending to non-banks that finance AI are the places it will ask about.

In an early trial of daraxonrasib, a daily pill that targets the RAS proteins behind most pancreatic cancers, given with chemotherapy to 61 people recently diagnosed with advanced, spread disease, tumours stopped growing in about 90%, shrank or disappeared in just over 60%, and became undetectable on CT scans in about 8% (one in 12), the BBC reports.
