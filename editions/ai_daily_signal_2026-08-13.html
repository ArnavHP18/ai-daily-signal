ChatGPT starts carrying ads — the free tier stops being a loss leader

OpenAI has begun testing advertising inside ChatGPT, and the rollout moved faster than the announcement. Ads are being served to Free and Go users in the US, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, and on 11 August the test expanded to the UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan and South Korea; paid plans see nothing (OpenAI, NBC News, ChatGPT Ads tracker). OpenAI's stated design constraints are specific: ads are labelled and visually separated from answers, they do not influence model outputs, conversations are not shared with marketers, and sensitive categories — politics, health, mental health — and under-age profiles are excluded. Users can view, dismiss or comment on what they are shown. The framing in OpenAI's own post is infrastructure economics: ads "help fund infrastructure work, supporting broader access to AI through higher quality free and low cost options" (MM+M). It lands in the same fortnight OpenAI took ChatGPT Go global (GSMArena) and days before the S-1 window this briefing has been tracking since 22 May.

So what: the free tier now has a second revenue line, which changes what "free" is allowed to cost — if you build on the consumer surface, assume ad inventory becomes a ranking-adjacent surface eventually, and if you buy media, a new labelled placement just opened in nine countries with no third-party measurement story yet.

It arrives alongside ChatGPT Go's global launch and inside the reported mid-to-late-August window for OpenAI's S-1 to flip public, which makes a second, non-subscription revenue line materially more than a product change.

IMPACT 7/10#4 BUSINESS · CHINA

Beijing forces Meta to unwind its $2B Manus deal — and user data gets deleted on 23 August

AI-agent company Manus said on 11 August it will resume operating as an independent company, roughly eight months after Meta acquired it for a reported $2B in late December 2025. China's NDRC blocked the acquisition in April 2026 and ordered it unwound, citing rules on outbound investment and technology export controls (Bloomberg, Reuters via Yahoo). The unwind has a user-facing cost: data generated by certain users on or after 29 December 2025 will be deleted between 23 August 08:00 and 24 August (SGT), with affected users notified in-app and by email and able to back up first (Manus).

A completed, paid-for acquisition was reversed by a regulator eight months on, and the reversal deletes customer data. If you run agent workloads on a Chinese-founded vendor, the exit risk is not just service continuity — put a data-export date in the contract.

IMPACT 6/10#6 RESEARCH · HEALTH

Google's AMIE runs real-time clinical video consultations — and rates on par with primary-care physicians

Today's research beat. Google published AMIE (Video), built on Gemini and Project Astra, which conducts synchronous clinical video consultations — reading non-verbal cues, guiding patient actors through virtual physical exams and reasoning diagnostically in real time — using an asynchronous multi-agent architecture because a single agent cannot hold conversational latency and deep reasoning at once (arXiv 2608.09861, Google Research). The study ran 100 clinical scenarios across three arms — AMIE Video, AMIE Text and PCP Video — over a Meet-like interface, with 15 trained actors across cardiopulmonary, abdominal, HEENT, neuro/psychiatric and musculoskeletal presentations. AMIE (Video) matched or exceeded PCPs on core clinical competencies; PCPs were preferred on rapport and partnership. Google says real-patient studies must come before any clinical conclusion (Google).

Actors are not patients and a rating is not an outcome — but the architecture is the transferable result: split perception, dialogue and reasoning across agents when latency and depth conflict.
