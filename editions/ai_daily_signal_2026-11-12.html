#1Models · new model types · 11 NovIMPACT 8/10

Google ships Gemini Decision 1, a model that decides instead of writing

Google DeepMind launched the Gemini Decisions API, powered by a new model, Gemini Decision 1 (gemini-decision-1). Instead of writing an answer token by token, it scores the options you give it in a single forward pass and returns probabilities and confidence scores. One request can carry one input of text, images, audio, video or PDFs (up to 256K tokens) plus up to 1,000 independent questions of three kinds: yes/no, choice and rubric scores. Google quotes 150-250 ms end to end, $0.10 per million input tokens and no output charge, says it leads its benchmark suite with the lowest calibration error, and says it is nearly 5x cheaper than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite on that suite. Vercel's AI Gateway listed it the same day. Simon Willison notes the API copies TypeSafe's Jev almost exactly (Google now joins Jev and OpenAI's decision model); Jev stays cheaper at 4.2 cents per million but handles only text.

So what: decision models are becoming a standard building block. For banks they fit routing, policy checks, document triage and fraud flags, where a calibrated probability and a fixed latency matter more than prose. Three vendors now sell the same shape of API, so switching costs are low.

Notion confirms Fireworks breach exposed AI chat transcripts

NOTABLETwo or more outletsSecurity · AI infrastructure · 11 Nov
WHAT CHANGED Notion's own disclosure: transcripts from its Insights dataset in a small number of workspaces were exposed; Insights disabled, Fireworks traffic cut
Notion disclosed that the breach at its AI infrastructure provider Fireworks AI exposed Notion AI chat transcripts in a small number of workspaces, BleepingComputer reports. The transcripts came from the dataset behind Insights, a feature that shows admins how teams use AI, which Notion has now disabled. Fireworks alerted Notion on 2 November about an exposed dataset it said held only synthetic test data; Notion cut traffic to Fireworks, deleted all its data from Fireworks' systems and halted training runs by 3 November; Fireworks confirmed on 8 November that customer data had been accessed. Fireworks runs some open-weight models in Notion AI.

So what: usage-analytics features quietly keep copies of prompts. Ask SaaS vendors which sub-processors hold chat logs, for how long, and for what features.
